Keep your mood chart with your medication and fill it in right after taking your medication so you do not forget. Comparing with the day before makes it easier to fill in. If you missed a day, fill it in as soon as you can.

1. Mood

In the evening, please mark a V in the box that most accurately expresses your mood as representative of the whole day.

Score 0: Your mood is typical and you feel at ease. You do everything you need to do. There is no change in how well you perform at work and no change in functioning.
Score -1: You have depressive symptoms such as feeling indifferent and somewhat low in motivation, but they hardly affect your ability to function at work. The depression you feel is clear to you, but people around you often do not realize you are depressed.
Score +1: You feel better than your usual good mood, your motivation increases, and at times you feel irritable, but people around you do not notice the change. If you concentrate, you can finish what you need to do without mistakes.
Score -2: You feel depressed and down, with a clear impact on your social life, so people around you notice that you are depressed. Your daily life is slightly disrupted, but with effort you can keep up your routine.
Score +2: Elevated symptoms are clear: you talk more and want to do more, are full of confidence, and are excessively irritable, so people around you tell you that you are different from usual. Your daily life is slightly disrupted, but with effort you can keep up your routine.
Score -3: You feel quite depressed and down. Your social life, such as going out and shopping, is clearly disrupted, and there are times when you cannot go to school or work or do housework. Even with effort, your ability to function at work is clearly reduced.
Score +3: With symptoms such as pushing yourself forward, feeling cocky, taking on more than you can handle, and exploding over trivial matters, you may quarrel with people around you or run into legal trouble. You spend more than you can afford or go off on impulsive trips.
Score -4: You feel extremely depressed or down. You cannot take care of yourself or do what you need to do at all, and because of severe anxiety, restlessness, and suicidal thoughts, you need care from others
Score +4: You feel extremely elevated and may even act violently toward people or objects. Hallucinations (hearing voices) or delusions are so severe that hospitalization is needed
Mixed state – When you have several different moods in a single day and cannot express them with one score, check “Mixed state” and record two scores: the highest mood score and the lowest

2. Emotional reactivity

Please record a score between -4 and +4. (The further toward -, the lower; the further toward +, the higher.)
Even if nothing happened that would trigger an emotional reaction, you can fill this in by thinking about how you would have reacted if it had.

Fear/timidity/
worry/anxiety
  • Take the way you usually feel when you are at ease as 0
  • +: You feel fearful, timid, worried, and anxious even about things you would not normally mind
  • +1: Things you would not normally mind stir up these feelings, but you do not show it
  • +2: You express these feelings in words or actions about things that bother you
  • +3: You express these feelings so openly that other people can easily tell
  • +4: You are so overwhelmed by these feelings that you cannot feel anything else
  • -: You cannot feel even the fear/timidity/worry/anxiety that you normally would
  • -1: You do not react to minor things that call for some attention and just let them pass
  • -2: Even more important things that call for attention feel like no big deal
  • -3: Even very important matters feel like no big deal at all
  • -4: Even threatening stimuli or events feel like no big deal at all
Irritability/anger
  • 0: Irritability and anger within a reasonable range
  • +: You become on edge and angry even about things you would normally let go.
  • +1: Things you would not normally mind start to bother you, but you do not show it
  • +2: You express it in words or actions about things that bother you
  • +3: You express it to the point of conflict with other people about things that bother you
  • +4: About things that bother you, you resort to violence, abusive language, or impulsive actions/thoughts
  • -: You feel nothing even about things anyone would get angry about.
  • -1: You do not react to minor stress and just let it pass
  • -2: You cannot express stress in words or actions even when you need to
  • -3: Even when there is conflict, you cannot speak up about things you should
  • -4: Even threatening stimuli or events feel like nothing and you do not react

Observe whether your mood symptoms and emotional reactivity move together in the same direction.

3. Motivation and thinking

Please record a score between -4 and +4. (The further toward -, the lower; the further toward +, the higher.)
Take the way you usually feel when you are at ease as 0.

Interest/
enjoyment/
spending/plans
  • Take the way you usually feel when you are at ease as 0
  • +: You have more interest than usual, enjoy the same things even more, spend more money or invest, and make more plans for work, hobbies, appointments, and so on
  • +1: Slightly more than usual
  • +2: Clearly more than usual
  • +3: Much more than usual, using a lot of time, energy, and money, which becomes a burden
  • +4: Using a very large amount of time, energy, and money, which becomes a heavy burden and causes difficulties
  • -: You have less interest than usual in what you do, no longer enjoy things you used to enjoy, spend less money, and think less about work, hobbies, and appointments
  • -1: Slightly less than usual
  • -2: Clearly less than usual
  • -3: Much less than usual, with very little time, energy, or money spent
  • -4: Almost no time, energy, or money is spent, and you pass the time feeling empty
Activity level
  • Take the way you usually feel when you are at ease as 0
  • +: You move around more and get more done than usual.
  • +1: Slightly more than usual
  • +2: Clearly more than usual
  • +3: Much more than usual, to the point of being a strain compared with your usual stamina
  • +4: So active that it is a heavy strain compared with your usual stamina, to the point of exhaustion
  • -: You move around less and get less done than usual.
  • -1: Slightly less than usual
  • -2: Clearly less than usual
  • -3: Much less than usual, with a very low activity level
  • -4: So inactive that you lie down almost all day
Speed and
amount of
thinking
  • Take the way you usually feel when you are at ease as 0
  • +: Thoughts speed up and follow one after another; many ideas come to mind, or you become absorbed in your thoughts. At times you talk more or write more
  • +1: Slightly more than usual
  • +2: Clearly more than usual
  • +3: Much more than usual, so that other people would have trouble keeping up if they listened
  • +4: So much more that even you cannot keep up, and you feel confused
  • -: Thoughts slow down and do not come to you. Speaking is not easy, and little comes to mind
  • -1: Slightly less than usual
  • -2: Clearly less than usual
  • -3: Much less than usual, so that others find you slow and frustrating
  • -4: So much less that communication is impossible and you have almost no thoughts
Content of thinking
  • Take the way you usually feel when you are at ease as 0
  • +: You are more optimistic than usual. You feel that everything will go well and you gain confidence. In severe cases, you feel that you are special
  • +1: Slightly more positive thoughts than usual
  • +2: Clearly more positive thoughts than usual
  • +3: Many more positive thoughts than usual, to the point that other people would think it excessive if they heard them
  • +4: So positive that you think everything will go well and that you are special and capable
  • -: You are more negative than usual. You feel that nothing will go well and your confidence drops. In severe cases, you feel guilty or want to die
  • -1: Slightly more negative thoughts than usual
  • -2: Clearly more negative thoughts than usual
  • -3: Many more negative thoughts than usual, to the point that other people would think it excessive if they heard them
  • -4: So negative that you think nothing goes well, life has no meaning, and you are incapable

For 4. Hours of sleep (hours), Weight (kg), and Alcohol (bottles/glasses), please write in the value yourself, using the unit shown.
For menstruation, please mark a v on every day from the first day to the last day of your period.
For 5. Alcohol, binge eating, pain and physical symptoms, crying, hyperventilation and panic attacks, please mark a v on every date when it occurred.
